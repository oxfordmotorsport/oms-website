Governing Documents

Club Documents

All documents are maintained under the University of Oxford’s Regulations for the Activities and Conduct of Student Members (Rules Committee Regulations 1 of 2017) made under Statute XI, and are reviewed at each annual re-registration. Questions about interpretation of the Constitution are settled by the Proctors (paragraph 34). For queries, contact the Secretary at secretary@oxfordmotorsport.co.uk.
