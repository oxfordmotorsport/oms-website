Oxford Motorsport Society

Join the Society

Annual membership is £5 and open to all University of Oxford students. Join online in a couple of minutes through our secure Stripe checkout.

Membership

What you get

Access to all events

Members get access to every watch party, speaker event, social, and circuit trip we run throughout the Oxford academic year.

Eligibility

Membership is open to all student members of the University of Oxford, including:

  • All undergraduate and postgraduate students of the University
  • Visiting students on the University’s Register of Visiting Students
  • Students at Permanent Private Halls and Ruskin / Ripon Colleges
  • Members of Oxford Brookes University (subject to the one-fifth cap in the Constitution)

Your membership register

Your name, college, and University email are held on the Club’s membership register as required by paragraph 21 of the Constitution. This register may be inspected by the University Proctors and Clubs Office on request. See our Privacy Notice for full details.

Code of Conduct

All members must agree to the Club’s Code of Conduct as a condition of membership.
