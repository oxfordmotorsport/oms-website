Membership

What you get

Events

Most of our events will be open to everyone. Members will get priority for special events.

How long membership lasts

One payment covers you for the whole of your course. If you go on to further study at Oxford, your membership carries on — there’s no need to rejoin.

Who can join

  • All undergraduate and postgraduate students of the University of Oxford, including visiting students
  • Students on diploma and certificate courses, at Permanent Private Halls, and at Ruskin and Ripon Colleges
  • Students at Oxford Brookes University (Brookes members may make up no more than one-fifth of the total membership, as set out in our Constitution)

Your membership record

We keep your name, college or university, and email on our membership register. See our Privacy Notice for full details.

Code of Conduct

All members must agree to the Club’s Code of Conduct as a condition of membership.
