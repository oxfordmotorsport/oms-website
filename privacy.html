Oxford Motorsport Society

Privacy Notice

How we collect, use, and protect your personal data in connection with OMS membership and activities.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

The Oxford Motorsport Society (“OMS”, “we”, “us”) is a student-run society in Oxford. For any data-related enquiries, email oxfordmotorsport@gmail.com.

This notice applies to personal data we collect when you apply for membership, attend our events, buy from our store, or otherwise interact with OMS.

2. What personal data we collect

When you join OMS through our Stripe checkout page, we collect:

  • Full name
  • College or university
  • University email address (used to confirm your eligibility)
  • Confirmation that you have agreed to the Code of Conduct
  • Payment confirmation reference from Stripe (we do not store your card details — these are handled by Stripe’s PCI-compliant systems)

When you buy event tickets or merchandise through our store, Stripe collects the details needed to complete your order (such as your name, email, and a delivery address for merchandise).

For event registrations or circuit trips we may additionally collect:

  • Dietary requirements or food allergies (where catering is provided)
  • Emergency contact details and relevant medical information (for overnight or overseas trips, provided voluntarily)

3. Why we collect it and our legal basis

We process your data for the following purposes:

  • Membership register — we keep a register of members so that we can administer membership and confirm eligibility. Our legal basis is the legitimate interests of the Club in administering its membership.
  • Communication about events — to tell you about our events. Our legal basis is legitimate interests (you joined specifically to receive this information).
  • Payments and orders — Stripe processes payments on our behalf and retains transaction records for their own legal obligations. We use order details only to fulfil your order and keep a payment confirmation for our accounts.
  • Event photographs — photos taken at our events may be published on this website and our social media, on the basis of the Club’s legitimate interest in promoting its activities. If you would prefer not to appear, tell the organiser at the event or email us and we will remove any published photo of you.
  • Safety and welfare — dietary and medical information is processed only for the specific event for which it was provided, on the basis of your consent.

4. Who sees your data

Access to member data is limited to:

  • The Club Committee, in the course of administering the Club
  • Stripe, as our payment processor, under their own Privacy Policy

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties for marketing purposes.

5. How long we keep it

Membership register data is kept for as long as you are a member (normally the length of your course, or longer if you continue studying in Oxford), and for a reasonable period afterwards for accounting purposes (typically one year). Payment records are retained for six years as required by accounting regulations. Dietary and medical information collected for a specific event is deleted after that event has taken place.

When Committee members hand over to their successors, personal data held by the outgoing officer is transferred to the incoming officer for the same purposes and is not retained by the outgoing officer personally.

6. Your rights

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to our need to keep a membership register while you are a member)
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, email oxfordmotorsport@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to keep member data secure. Membership details are stored in the Club’s Stripe account, which is accessible only to the Committee members who administer membership (normally the Secretary and Treasurer), and are not published publicly. Payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe; OMS does not receive or store payment card details.

8. Complaints and formal records

Under the Complaints Procedure, a confidential record of any formal complaints and their outcomes is kept by the Secretary in accordance with the Data Protection Act 2018.

9. Changes to this notice

This notice is reviewed at least once a year. Material changes will be notified to members by email. The current version is always available at this URL.

10. Contact

For any data protection enquiries, email oxfordmotorsport@gmail.com.
